# Risk levels, risk identification rules, and approval processes

This topic introduces how to manage risk levels, risk identification rules, and approval processes.

## Background information

- Risk level: the risk level of a ticket, which determines the approval process of a ticket.

- Risk identification rule: a rule that determines the risk level of a ticket.

- Approval process: a change task process defined by the ODC system administrator.

## Principle

![1](https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/420/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/1.0EN.png)

1. The ODC system administrator selects the approval nodes and the validity period when creating an approval process.

2. The ODC system administrator configures approval processes for each risk level (high, medium, and low).

3. The ODC system administrator configures risk identification rules for each risk level (high, medium, and low).

4. When an ODC user initiates a task ticket, ODC determines the risk level based on the risk identification rules.

5. ODC calls the corresponding approval process based on the risk level.

6. The approval role on the approval node approves or rejects the task ticket.

## Prerequisites

You have the system administrator role.

## Approval process management

1. In the project collaboration window, choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.page.Secure.SafetySpecifications">Security Specifications</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="odc.component.ResourceSelector.const.RiskLevel">Risk Level</UI-TERM>**. Then choose the target risk level and click **<UI-TERM key="odc.components.RolePage.Editing">Edit</UI-TERM>** next to the approval process.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/2.1EN.png" width="400">

2. In the **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.ChooseTheApprovalProcess">Select Approval Process</UI-TERM>** drop-down list of the pop-up dialog box, click **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.ManagementApprovalProcess">Manage approval process</UI-TERM>**.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/2.2EN.png" width="400">

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.ManagementApprovalProcess.1">Manage approval process</UI-TERM>** window, click **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.NewApprovalProcess">New Approval Process</UI-TERM>**.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/2.3EN.png" width="400">

4. In the **<UI-TERM key="odc.component.FormModal.CreateAnApprovalProcess">Create Approval Process</UI-TERM>** window, specify the following parameters.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/2.4EN.png" width="900">

   | Parameter | Description |
   |-------------|--------------|
   | Process Name | The name of the task process.  |
   | Set Approval Nodes | Select approval roles.  |
   | Process Validity Period | The validity period for approval and execution. If the execution times out, the task will expire.  |

5. Click **<UI-TERM key="odc.components.RecordPage.Create">Create</UI-TERM>** to create the approval process.

6. In the approval process list, you can edit or delete a created approval process.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/2.5EN.png" width="400">

## Risk level management

On the project collaboration page, choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.page.Secure.SafetySpecifications">Security Specifications</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.component.Task.PartitionTask.DetailContent.RiskLevel">Risk Level</UI-TERM>**. On the page that appears, you can configure the high-risk, medium-risk, and low-risk approval processes, as well as the risk identification rules.

<img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/3EN.png" width="900">

## Risk identification rule management

1. On the project collaboration page, choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.page.Secure.SafetySpecifications">Security Specifications</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="odc.RiskLevel.components.ViewRiskLevelDrawer.RiskLevel">Risk Level</UI-TERM>**. Then choose the target risk level and click **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.NewRules">New Rule</UI-TERM>**.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/4EN.png" width="400">

2. Click **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.AddConditions.1">Add Condition</UI-TERM>** or **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.page.Secure.RiskLevel.components.AddConditionGroup">Add Condition Group</UI-TERM>** to add one or more condition rules.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/5EN.png" width="400">

3. Click **<UI-TERM key="odc.component.CreateFunctionModal.ConfirmToClose">Confirm</UI-TERM>** to add the risk identification rules.

4. In the risk identification rule list, you can view, edit, or delete the rules.

   <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/433/700.database-change-management/300.risk-level-risk-identification-rules-and-approval-process/6EN.png" width="900">
