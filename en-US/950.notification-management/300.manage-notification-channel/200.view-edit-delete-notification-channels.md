# View, edit, and delete a notification channel

This topic describes how to view, edit, and delete a notification channel.

## View a notification channel

1. Log in to the OceanBase Developer Center (ODC) console. Choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.SpaceContainer.Sider.Project">Projects</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>**. On the **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>** tab, click **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.87BBE655">Notification Channels</UI-TERM>** in the left-side navigation pane.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/200.view-notification-channel/1EN.png" width="900">

2. In the notification channel list, click **<UI-TERM key="odc.TaskManagePage.component.TaskTools.View">View</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.components.550A0B74">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of a notification channel to view its detailed configurations.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/200.view-notification-channel/2EN.png">

## Edit a notification channel

1. Log in to the ODC console. Choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.SpaceContainer.Sider.Project">Projects</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>**. On the **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>** tab, click **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.87BBE655">Notification Channels</UI-TERM>** in the left-side navigation pane.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/200.view-notification-channel/1EN.png" width="900">

2. In the notification channel list, click **<UI-TERM key="odc.ExternalIntegration.SqlInterceptor.Edit">Edit</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="odc.components.SensitiveRule.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of a notification channel to edit its configurations.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/200.view-notification-channel/3EN.png" width="900">

## Delete a notification channel

1. Log in to the ODC console. Choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.SpaceContainer.Sider.Project">Projects</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>**. On the **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>** tab, click **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.87BBE655">Notification Channels</UI-TERM>** in the left-side navigation pane.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/200.view-notification-channel/1EN.png" width="900">

2. In the notification channel list, click **<UI-TERM key="odc.src.store.snippet.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="src.component.Task.component.CommonDetailModal.EDEF0329">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of a notification channel to delete it.

3. In the confirmation dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="odc.TreeNodeMenu.config.view.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/200.view-notification-channel/4EN.png">
