# Manage notification rules

This topic describes how to view and edit notification rules.

## View notification rules

Log in to the OceanBase Developer Center (ODC) console. Choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.SpaceContainer.Sider.Project">Projects</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>**. On the **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>** tab, click **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.25A341FB">Notification Rules</UI-TERM>** in the left-side navigation pane. Then, you can view the supported ticket events and the enabling status and notification channels of the ticket events.

<img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/432/950.notification/1.png" width="900">

## Edit a notification rule

1. Log in to the ODC console. Choose **<UI-TERM key="odc.SpaceContainer.Sider.Project">Projects</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>**. On the **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.B4D9BC23">Notification</UI-TERM>** tab, click **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.25A341FB">Notification Rules</UI-TERM>** in the left-side navigation pane.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/432/950.notification/2.png" width="900">

2. In the notification rule list, click &ensp;<img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/400.manage-notification-rules/0.png" width="16" height="17"> to search for a ticket event and then click **<UI-TERM key="src.page.Project.Notification.components.7555452E">Add Channel</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="odc.SensitiveColumn.components.ScanForm.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of the ticket event to configure a notification channel.

    <img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/432/950.notification/3.png" width="400">

3. Click &ensp;<img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/odc/431/950.notification-management/400.manage-notification-rules/3.png" width="20" height="13"> to enable the notification rule.
